Stay Safe Online

Practical advice and trusted resources to help keep you safe online.

Why Cyber Safety Matters

Cyber criminals are constantly looking for opportunities to steal passwords, financial information and personal data. Taking a few simple precautions can significantly reduce your risk.

Quick Safety Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Think before clicking links or opening attachments.
  • Keep your devices and software up to date.
  • Verify unexpected requests for money or information.
  • Use reputable antivirus and security software.

Report Something Suspicious

If you receive a suspicious email, text message, phone call, or website, report it to your IT team or security contact immediately.
